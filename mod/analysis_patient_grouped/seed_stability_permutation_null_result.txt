Interpretation guide:
- If permuted-label test acc collapses to ~0.25, permutation worked as intended (sanity check).
- High seed-stability correlation/Jaccard (real labels) = attention pattern is reproducible,
  a necessary (not sufficient) condition for trusting it as interpretation.
- High real-vs-permuted correlation/Jaccard = attention looks similar regardless of whether
  labels are real or nonsense, i.e. driven by graph/data structure rather than the learned
  task -- this would undermine using attention as a circuit-level explanation, independent
  of the causal-ablation finding (attention_rollout_and_causal_ablation.py) that top-attention
  edges are not causally load-bearing either.
